Not sent by this meter

Fields a Dutch DSMR 5 meter includes but this one leaves out:

  • Power-outage counters and outage log (96.7.21, 96.7.9, 99.97.0)
  • Voltage dip and spike counters (32.32.0, 32.36.0)
  • Per-phase power (21.7.0, 22.7.0)
  • Gas or water meter readings (0-n:24.2.1)
  • Text code (96.13.1). The text message field 96.13.0 is sent but empty.

Resolution

  • Energy counters: 1 Wh
  • Power: 10 W steps
  • Voltage: shown to 0.1 V, changes in whole volts
  • Current: 0.1 A
